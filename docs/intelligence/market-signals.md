# Market Signals

> **Status: Future**

Market Signals are concise, data-derived indicators intended to make complex activity easier to understand.

Examples may include Rising Activity, Collector Accumulation, Increasing Participation, Declining Activity, High Wallet Concentration, New Collector Growth and Post-Launch Momentum.

These are **information signals, not buy/sell signals**.

Each signal should eventually expose its data basis, time window and limitations.
