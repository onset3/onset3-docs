# ONSET3 Intelligence

> **Status: Future**

ONSET3 Intelligence is the future data layer for understanding NFT markets, projects, launches and collector behavior.

The purpose is not to produce generic dashboards or speculative buy/sell calls. It is to convert measurable activity into useful context.

The intelligence layer covers NFT market intelligence, collector behavior, collection/project signals, launch performance, chain activity, market trends, market signals and intelligence reports.
