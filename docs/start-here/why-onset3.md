# Why ONSET3?

> **Status: Current**

NFT discovery is fragmented across social feeds, project websites, community servers, marketplaces and private bookmarks.

This creates recurring problems:

- launches are easy to miss;
- allowlist access is difficult to organize;
- project information is scattered;
- official destinations can be unclear;
- collectors often rely on screenshots, bookmarks and multiple calendars;
- useful market context is separated from the actions collectors actually take.

ONSET3 is designed to reduce that fragmentation by combining discovery, tracking, participation and eventually intelligence in one product.
