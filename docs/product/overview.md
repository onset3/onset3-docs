# Product Overview

> **Status: Current**

ONSET3 is organized around five product actions:

- **Discover** relevant NFT projects and launches.
- **Track** personal interest, access and time-sensitive activity.
- **Participate** in events and campaigns.
- **Manage** project presence and submissions.
- **Verify** official information and destinations.

The product is designed to grow without collapsing every concept into one feed. Projects, launches, events, campaigns, wallets and portfolio data are separate entities with different purposes.
