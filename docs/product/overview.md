# Product Overview

> **Status: Current + Roadmap**

ONSET3 is organized around five product actions:

- **Discover** relevant NFT projects and launches.
- **Track** personal interest and launch dates with Watchlist and Calendar. Personal allowlist tracking is planned.
- **Participate** in events and campaigns — planned, not currently available.
- **Manage** project presence and submissions.
- **Verify** project information through editorial review. Onchain verification is not available.

The product is designed to grow without collapsing every concept into one feed. Projects and launches are implemented; events, campaigns, wallets and portfolio data describe planned product concepts with different purposes.
