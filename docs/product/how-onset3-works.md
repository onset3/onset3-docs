# How ONSET3 Works

> **Status: Current + Roadmap**

A typical collector journey is:

```text
Discover
  ↓
Project
  ↓
Launch or Event
  ↓
Watch / Track / Save
  ↓
Calendar or Reminder
  ↓
Participate
  ↓
Portfolio
  ↓
Intelligence
```

The early product is focused on the upper part of this flow. Portfolio, participation and intelligence expand the same journey rather than creating unrelated products.
