# Product Entities

> **Status: Current + Roadmap**

Projects, launches and users are implemented. Events, campaigns, wallets, portfolio and intelligence signals below describe planned product concepts, not available features.

## Project
The persistent identity of a project.

## Launch
A time-based launch, mint, claim or release associated with a project.

## Event
A scheduled activity associated with a project or community.

## Campaign
A structured participation experience containing tasks and progress.

## User
An ONSET3 account used for personal tracking and project management.

## Wallet
A blockchain address added for portfolio visibility.

## Portfolio
An aggregated view of NFT holdings and later activity/analytics.

## Intelligence Signal
A future derived market or behavior signal based on measurable data.
