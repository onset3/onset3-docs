# FAQ

> **Status: Current**

## Is ONSET3 an NFT marketplace?
No. The core product is focused on discovery, tracking, participation, portfolio visibility and future intelligence.

## Does verification mean a project is safe?
No. Verification describes reviewed information within a defined scope. It is not an endorsement or financial guarantee.

## Is Portfolio live?
Portfolio is documented as a next-stage product layer unless its status is updated on the Portfolio pages.

## Are Campaigns financial reward systems?
The initial Campaign direction is task-based participation without assuming financial custody, prize smart contracts or a token economy.

## Does ONSET3 provide investment advice?
No. Market and intelligence features are intended to provide data and context, not individualized investment advice.
